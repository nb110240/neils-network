Features

Everything your network needs

Built for people who meet a lot of people and want to keep every relationship alive.

🧠

Natural Language Input

Just type what you remember. AI parses name, company, context, and next steps from freeform text.

🟢

Health Scores

Every contact gets a color-coded score. Green means active. Red means you're about to lose touch. No relationship slips through.

🔍

Semantic Search

"Who do I know in healthcare AI?" Search by meaning, not just keywords. Find the right person in seconds.

📨

Daily Digest

Every morning: "These 3 relationships need attention." Each with context and a one-click link to log an interaction.

📥

CSV & Gmail Import

Bring your existing network in seconds. Upload a CSV or pull directly from Google Contacts. Auto-maps columns.

🔥

"Going Cold" Dashboard

See every fading relationship at a glance. Sorted by urgency. One click to reconnect.

📅

Google Calendar Sync

Had a 1:1 coffee chat or meeting? Savvo detects it and adds the person as a contact automatically. Your network grows while you work.
