**Tweet 4 (What you built) — 📸 SCREENSHOT: /add page with the text input:**

Meet Savvo — an AI relationship manager.

You type messy notes like:
"Met Sarah at TechCrunch. She runs a fintech startup, looking for a head of sales. Intro'd by Mike. Should follow up next week."

AI extracts: name, company, role, how you met, and next steps.

No forms. No fields. Just type what you remember.

**Tweet 5 (Health scores) — 📸 SCREENSHOT: dashboard showing health score colors + Reach Out Today list:**

Every contact gets a health score.

🟢 Green = active (talked recently)
🟡 Yellow = cooling (31-90 days)
🟠 Orange = going cold (91-180 days)
🔴 Red = at risk (180+ days)

My dashboard tells me exactly who needs attention today. Sorted by urgency.

No more "I should probably reach out to..." guilt.
